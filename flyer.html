CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Alex Carter

Senior Security Engineer

Alex Carter is a Senior Security Engineer working on cloud infrastructure security. Alex has spoken at multiple regional conferences on topics ranging from container security to zero-trust architecture. With a background in distributed systems, Alex brings a practitioner's perspective to securing complex multi-cloud deployments — focusing on what actually works in production, not just in theory.

When not on stage, Alex contributes to open-source tooling in the cloud-native security ecosystem and mentors junior engineers entering the security field.
