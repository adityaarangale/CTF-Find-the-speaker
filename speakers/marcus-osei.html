CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Marcus Osei

DevSecOps Architect, Veritelum

Marcus Osei designs and implements security pipelines at Veritelum, where his work focuses on software supply-chain integrity — SBOM generation, dependency auditing, build attestation, and runtime policy enforcement at scale. He has spent the last seven years embedding security into CI/CD workflows across engineering teams of 10 to 300 developers.

Prior to Veritelum, Marcus worked at a cloud-native consultancy helping fintech startups achieve SOC 2 compliance without grinding engineering velocity to a halt. He has a strong opinion that most supply-chain security failures are a tooling UX problem as much as a technical one, and his talk will make the case with real examples.

Marcus co-organises a regional DevSecOps meetup and occasionally writes long-form articles on pipeline architecture for internal engineering blogs.

Talk

"Securing CI/CD: From SBOM to Runtime Attestation"

Marcus writes internally at Veritelum and does not maintain a public blog or open-source presence. Slide deck will be shared via the conference portal after the event.

Two days of deep technical talks, workshops, and networking for the security community.
Pune International Convention Centre · March 14–15, 2026.
