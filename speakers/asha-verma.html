CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Asha Verma

CISO, Klayton Financial

Asha Verma is the Chief Information Security Officer at Klayton Financial, a Series C fintech operating across South Asia and Southeast Asia. She joined Klayton at the seed stage and has built the security function from the ground up — spanning product security, compliance, incident response, and security culture across an engineering organisation of 180 people.

Before Klayton, Asha held senior security roles at two large private-sector banks, where she led PCI-DSS certification programmes and built out 24/7 SOC capabilities. She holds a CISSP and an MBA, and believes most security programmes fail not from lack of tooling but from lack of organisational buy-in.

Asha is a board advisor for two early-stage security startups and speaks regularly on the intersection of security governance and engineering culture.

Talk

"Building a Risk-First Security Programme at Fintech Speed"

Asha is on ProNet but keeps her profile private. No public code repositories or personal blog.

Two days of deep technical talks, workshops, and networking for the security community.
