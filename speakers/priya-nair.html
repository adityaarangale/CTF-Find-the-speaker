CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Priya Nair

Principal Threat Analyst, SecureAxis

Priya Nair leads the threat intelligence practice at SecureAxis, where she specialises in tracking advanced persistent threat groups operating across financial services, government, and critical infrastructure sectors. With over ten years in adversarial research, Priya has built and led red-team operations that have uncovered campaign infrastructure spanning four continents.

Before SecureAxis, Priya spent five years at a national cybersecurity agency conducting malware reverse engineering and attribution analysis. She holds a master's degree in information security and is a frequent speaker at private intelligence-sharing forums. Her research on APT lateral movement techniques has been cited across the industry.

Outside of work, Priya mentors women entering the security field and contributes to curriculum development for university-level threat-intelligence courses.

Talk

"Hunting APTs in Hybrid Cloud Environments"

Priya prefers to keep a low public profile. No public social media or code repositories — all research is published through SecureAxis's official threat intelligence blog.

Two days of deep technical talks, workshops, and networking for the security community.
