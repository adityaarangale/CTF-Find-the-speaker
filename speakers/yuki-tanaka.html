CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Yuki Tanaka

Security Researcher, KairenLabs

Yuki Tanaka is a security researcher at KairenLabs specialising in low-level hardware and hypervisor vulnerabilities. Their work centres on side-channel attack vectors — Spectre-class vulnerabilities, cache timing attacks, and cross-VM information leakage in public cloud multi-tenant environments. Yuki has disclosed CVEs affecting three major hypervisor platforms and contributed to coordinated disclosure processes with cloud vendors.

Before KairenLabs, Yuki completed a PhD in computer architecture at a research university in Tokyo, focusing on microarchitectural side channels. Their dissertation work directly informed several hardware mitigations shipped in modern processor generations.

Yuki presents at academic and industry venues and maintains a deep interest in the gap between what hardware vendors document and what hardware actually does.

Talk

"Side-Channel Attacks in Cloud Multi-Tenancy"

Yuki's published papers are available through KairenLabs' research portal. No public social media profiles.
