CyberTech Summit 2026 Speaker

Daniel Cho

Staff Engineer, CipherNova

Daniel Cho is a Staff Engineer at CipherNova, where he leads the cryptographic infrastructure team responsible for securing high-throughput payment systems processing over 40 million transactions daily. His work spans protocol design, key management architecture, HSM integration, and ensuring PCI-DSS compliance without sacrificing system performance.

Daniel previously held engineering roles at two payments infrastructure companies, where he developed a deep specialisation in authenticated encryption, certificate lifecycle management, and the surprisingly hard problem of rotating keys in distributed systems without downtime.

He holds a BSc in mathematics and a postgraduate certificate in information security. He is a quiet but prolific contributor to IETF working groups on cryptographic protocol standards.

Talk

"Cryptographic Engineering at Scale: Keys, Certs, and the Things That Go Wrong"

Daniel does not maintain public social profiles. IETF contributions are published under the standards body's document archive.

Two days of deep technical talks, workshops, and networking for the security community.
Pune International Convention Centre · March 14–15, 2026.
