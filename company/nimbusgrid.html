About Us

NimbusGrid Technologies is a cloud infrastructure security company founded in 2019, helping enterprises secure multi-cloud deployments. We specialise in zero-trust network architecture, cloud workload protection, identity and access management, and runtime threat detection across AWS, GCP, and Azure environments.

Our engineering-first culture means we build tools that developers actually want to use. Security shouldn't be a gate at the end of the software lifecycle — it should be woven into every pipeline, every service mesh, every access policy from day one.

Headquartered in Bangalore with teams across Pune, Hyderabad, and Singapore, NimbusGrid serves mid-market and enterprise clients across APAC and EMEA. We are Series B funded and growing our product and engineering teams.

Our logo represents the intersection of cloud infrastructure (the cloud form) and interconnected security (the grid of nodes). You may recognise this from our stickers — we hand them out at every conference we attend.
